The Pronoun Quest

Explore grammar, solve challenges, and level up your English skills!

🎮 Interactive Grammar Game | Year 4

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this adventure, pupils will be able to:

  • Identify common pronouns correctly.
  • Choose suitable pronouns inside interactive sentences.
  • Create wonderful original sentences using pronouns.

📘 Mission 1: Learn the Grammar

A pronoun is a super-word used to replace a noun so we don't repeat names!

Subject pronouns: I, you, he, she, it, we, they

Object pronouns: me, you, him, her, it, us, them

Possessive adjectives: my, your, his, her, its, our, their

Without pronoun: Aina has a book. Aina reads Aina's book.

With pronoun: Aina has a book. She reads her book.

🔎 Mission 2: Sentence Detective

Clue: Adam is my friend. He is very kind.

🎮 Mission 3: Quiz Quest

Aina is my cousin. ___ lives near my house.
